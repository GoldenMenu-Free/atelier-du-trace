Questions

How do I get my workbook?

After paying on Payhip, you receive an email with a download link. The file is a PDF you keep.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes, as many times as you like for personal, family or classroom use.

Which paper size?

Each workbook comes in US Letter and A4.

Do the workbooks replace a professional?

No. They are practice tools. For significant difficulties, an occupational therapist, speech therapist or adult literacy tutor is the right person to see.
