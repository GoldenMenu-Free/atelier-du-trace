How to hold a pencil

The right grip, good posture and a well-placed sheet of paper: three simple adjustments that prevent fatigue and make writing smoother, for kids and adults alike.

Download the free printable guide

1. The three-finger grip

  • Don’t squeeze: your fingertips shouldn’t turn white.
  • Keep the wrist resting and in line with the forearm.
  • The fingers move; the forearm glides across the table.
  • A four-finger grip is also fine if it feels comfortable.

2. Good posture

3. Paper position

  • Paper tilted to the left, 20 to 30°
  • Paper in front of you, slightly right
  • Forearm in line with the paper
  • Paper tilted to the right, 30 to 35°
  • Paper slightly to the left
  • Keep the hand below the writing line
  • Forearm in line with the paper
